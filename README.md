# Fatsecret (fatsecret)
Fatsecret is an online platform that helps users track their diets, monitor their exercise routines, and achieve their health and fitness goals. Users can input their meals and snacks to receive a breakdown of calories, macronutrients, and other nutritional information. Additionally, Fatsecret offers a range of tools and resources to help users stay motivated and make informed choices about their health and wellness journey. By providing personalized recommendations and support, Fatsecret empowers individuals to take control of their health and make positive changes in their lifestyle.
